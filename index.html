My Favorite Kpop group

NewJeans is a five-member girl group formed by ADOR in 2022, consisting of members Minji, Hanni, Danielle, Haerin, and Hyein. They debuted in July 2022 with the single "Attention" and gained significant attention for their music, which fuses old-school R&B with catchy melodies. The group became known for hits like "Hype Boy" and "Super Shy". In November 2024, they announced their departure from ADOR, a subsidiary of the larger company HYBE, following a public dispute over contract terms and treatment.
